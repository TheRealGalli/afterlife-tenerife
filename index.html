// 01. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & VISION

From Digital Legend to Physical Reality

Led by project proponent Carlo Galli, the central goal is to elevate Afterlife from Cyberpunk 2077's digital icon into an international permanent entertainment flagship. Not a temporary pop-up, but a physical destination fusing gaming culture and high-end underground nightlife.

ZONE 01

The Immersive Pub

Faithful replica of Night City's legendary venue. An iconic gathering point for gamers, cyberpunk fans, and tourists featuring stainless steel counters, custom neon lighting, and a signature drink menu inspired by in-game legends.

ZONE 02

Main Room / Technological Club

State-of-the-art clubbing space dedicated to underground techno and industrial music. Designed to host world-renowned DJs and futuristic visual productions featuring holograms, interactive LED walls, and industrial staging.

WHY TENERIFE SOUTH?

The Strategic Permanent Hub

Unlike strictly seasonal destinations (e.g., Ibiza), Tenerife guarantees a constant international tourist stream 365 days a year, competitive real estate overheads, and the ideal launchpad to scale the format to London, Tokyo, and Berlin.

  • Constant tourist stream with 365-day operation
  • Advantageous tax regime & sustainable operational costs
  • Strategic bridge connecting Europe, Africa, and the Americas

// 03. LEADERSHIP & RECORD LABEL SYNERGY

Afterlife Records x Roccia Music

Project proponent Carlo Galli is the owner of the historic brand and domain rocciamusic.com. Leveraging this asset, Afterlife will not only be a venue, but an official record label co-branded with CDPR.

Modeled after the global success of Rockstar Games with Circoloco Records, Afterlife Records will produce underground cyberpunk techno releases, host global label nights, and drive streaming and sync licensing revenues.
